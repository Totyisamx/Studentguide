About Student Guide

A student project built with passion — to help Kuwait's graduates find their way.

Why This Project Exists

Choosing a university is one of the most important decisions a young person can make — yet many Kuwaiti students feel lost, overwhelmed, or underinformed when the time comes.

Student Guide was created to solve that problem. Built as a graduation project by Abdulrahman Abu Dakar, this platform brings together all the information students need — from university overviews and major requirements to fees and AI-powered recommendations — in one clean, accessible place.

The goal is simple: empower every Kuwaiti high school graduate with the knowledge to make a confident, informed decision about their academic future.

This website represents months of research, design, and development — and a deep belief that accessible information can change lives.
